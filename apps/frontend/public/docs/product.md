# Product

AI-native investment intelligence and workflow infrastructure. A five-layer architecture: connected data, intelligence, workflows, AI, and human decision — one intelligent layer across the investment lifecycle.

## Connected data

Your existing systems become inputs. The platform connects CRM, email, cloud storage, data rooms, spreadsheets, financial systems, and research so information flows into one connected model instead of being re-entered by hand.

## Intelligence

Companies, deals, relationships, and financials are structured and enriched as they arrive. Intelligence is built from context — firmographic, financial, and market data assembled before a human opens the record.

## Workflows

The investment lifecycle — origination, screening, underwriting, due diligence, IC, transaction execution, and portfolio management — runs on configurable workflows. Each stage is organized around how your firm actually invests.

## AI

AI reads, extracts, analyzes, and automates across the platform: research briefs, CIM analysis, financial review, diligence requests, memo drafting, IC briefing, and portfolio monitoring. Every output is designed to be verifiable.

## Human decision

AI prepares and analyzes. Investment judgment belongs to the humans. Conclusions link to evidence, evidence links to sources, and sources link to the document and page they came from.
