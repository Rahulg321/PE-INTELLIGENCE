# AI-native infrastructure for investment teams

An AI-native investment intelligence and workflow platform that connects your firm's data, workflows, and investment knowledge in one intelligent layer across the deal lifecycle.

## The problem

Investment decisions are built across fragmented systems. The information behind every decision lives in a dozen places: CRM, email, Excel, PDFs, data rooms, research, financials, notes, and internal knowledge. Turning it into a decision means manual assembly, copying, analysis, memo writing, and answering the same questions twice.

## The lifecycle

The platform is one intelligence layer across the investment lifecycle — origination, screening, underwriting, due diligence, investment committee, transaction execution, and portfolio management. Every stage is configurable around how your firm invests.

## Product surfaces

- **Pipeline and deal management** — companies, deals, and relationships tracked from source to decision.
- **AI research and analysis** — AI research that reads CIMs, screens deals, analyzes financials, and prepares analysis.
- **Institutional memory** — every thesis, rejected deal, finding, and IC decision preserved so the firm compounds intelligence.

## AI that understands the work

Not a blank chatbot. AI operates inside your deal context, criteria, documents, permissions, and workflow state. It researches companies and markets, analyzes CIMs, screens deals against criteria, identifies missing information, analyzes financial statements, generates diligence requests, drafts investment memos, prepares IC materials, monitors portfolio performance, and surfaces changes and risks.

The principle: AI prepares and analyzes. Humans decide.

## Configurable investment logic

Define investment criteria, thesis, screening rules, diligence requirements, IC requirements, and portfolio KPIs. The platform turns them into rules, workflows, and AI actions that apply consistently — without hard-coding any single firm's process.

## Why it is different

Not another AI chatbot. Not another rigid tool. It is an intelligence and workflow layer for the investment process.
