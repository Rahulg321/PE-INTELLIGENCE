# Integrations

Your investment stack should work together. From existing systems to one intelligence layer — the platform is built around the stack you already have.

## Integration categories

- **CRM** — sync companies, deals, contacts, and activities so your CRM stays the system of record, and the platform reads from it. (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Attio, Close)
- **Email** — connect mailboxes so inbound deal flow, correspondence, and intermediary notes become structured records. (Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook)
- **Cloud Storage** — index documents where they already live without forcing a migration. (Google Drive, SharePoint, Dropbox, Box, OneDrive)
- **Data Rooms** — connect diligence rooms so requested documents and their contents flow into the workstreams that need them. (Intralinks, Datasite, DocSend, iDeals, DealRoom)
- **Financial Systems** — bring actuals and projections in from accounting and FP&A tools. (QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Anaplan, Adaptive)
- **Market Data** — enrich companies and markets with firmographic and financial data. (PitchBook, Crunchbase, S&P Capital IQ, ZoomInfo)
- **Research** — keep market research, industry reports, and sector notes connected to the deals they inform.
- **Communication** — capture meeting notes and call transcripts so decisions are preserved. (Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack)
- **Spreadsheets** — map existing trackers and models as inputs so the platform can read them without replacing them. (Excel, Google Sheets, CSV imports)
- **APIs** — a documented API and webhooks for proprietary sourcing and internal systems. (REST API, webhooks, bulk import)

## Statuses are honest

Nothing is claimed as "Available" until it actually ships. Integration statuses — available, beta, or coming soon — are reported as they are.
