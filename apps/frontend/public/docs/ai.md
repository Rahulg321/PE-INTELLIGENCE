# AI

AI agents built around investment work. Each agent has a specific input, action, output, and human review step — built to be verified, not trusted blindly.

## Specific agents, specific outputs

- **Deal Research Agent** — takes a company, market, or sector and assembles a structured research brief with sources. The team reviews the brief and decides what to pursue.
- **Screening Agent** — takes a new opportunity and evaluates it against the firm's encoded criteria, surfacing red flags and unknowns. The team verifies the evidence and advances or passes.
- **CIM Agent** — reads a Confidential Information Memorandum and produces a company analysis, key questions, and red flags, each tied to the section it came from.
- **Financial Agent** — normalizes historical and LTM financials, analyzes trends and margins, and flags anomalies. The team reviews the normalization and decides which anomalies matter.
- **Diligence Agent** — reads across workstreams and documents, surfacing missing items, contradictions, and findings linked to their evidence. The team triages and assigns owners.
- **IC Agent** — drafts a decision-ready briefing — company, thesis, financials, valuation, risks, mitigants, returns — with every conclusion traced to its source. The committee decides.
- **Portfolio Agent** — monitors actual performance against the underwritten case and surfaces changes, variances, and covenant risk. The team reviews alerts and acts.

## The principle

AI prepares and analyzes. Humans decide.
