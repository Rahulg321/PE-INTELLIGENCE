# Underwriting

Underwriting means developing the investment case for a company. The platform brings the company, financials, market, management, transaction, and valuation into one connected workspace so the thesis is built from complete context.

The investment thesis becomes a living document — built from assumptions, pressure-tested across scenarios, and traceable to its sources.

## Pipeline

Company → Financials → Market → Management → Valuation → Assumptions → Returns → Risks → Thesis

## Capabilities

- **Financial analysis** — LTM and historical financials are normalized and analyzed for trends, margins, and anomalies — not transcribed by hand.
- **Historical trends** — revenue, EBITDA, margin, and cash flow trends presented across periods so the trajectory is visible at a glance.
- **Customer analysis** — concentration, retention, and growth drivers brought together from documents and data.
- **Market research** — market size, growth, competitive dynamics, and positioning assembled into the underwriting context.
- **Management assessment** — management track record and fit documented in one place, linked to the evidence behind each observation.
- **Valuation & returns** — transaction assumptions, debt structure, returns, and sensitivity analysis organized around your underwriting methodology.
- **Scenario analysis** — base, upside, and downside cases built on explicit assumptions so every outcome can be revisited.
- **Thesis capture** — the investment thesis is recorded alongside its supporting evidence — the case the firm is actually buying.

## The principle

Assumptions are made explicit, scenarios are pressure-tested, and the thesis is recorded with the evidence that supports it — so the case can be defended at IC and revisited after closing.
