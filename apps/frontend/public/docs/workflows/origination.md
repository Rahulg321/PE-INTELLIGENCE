# Origination

Capture opportunities without forcing your team to manually re-enter information. Your existing systems become inputs — the platform turns fragmented deal flow into structured companies, deals, and relationships.

Your existing systems become inputs. You do not need to migrate your entire CRM into the platform.

## Pipeline

Input → Company → Deal → Enrichment → Relationship → Screening → Pipeline

## Capabilities

- **Company creation** — a new opportunity becomes a company record with the facts that matter, pulled together from wherever the opportunity arrived.
- **Deal creation** — each opportunity is tracked as its own deal, with source, status, and economics — separate from the facts of the business.
- **Enrichment** — companies are enriched with firmographic, financial, and market context so you start with signal rather than a blank page.
- **Contact mapping** — owners, intermediaries, operators, and introducers are linked to the deal so the relationship map is visible at a glance.
- **Intermediary tracking** — track who introduced what, exclusivity windows, and correspondence with each intermediary.
- **Relationship intelligence** — see the full relationship context — who knows whom, prior interactions, and past deals — before you reach out.
- **Sourcing attribution** — every opportunity keeps its source, so you can measure which channels actually produce deals worth underwriting.
- **Automated research** — AI assembles an initial research brief on each new company, market, and management team before a human ever opens the record.

## The principle

Capture once, enrich automatically, and let every downstream stage start from context instead of a blank spreadsheet.
