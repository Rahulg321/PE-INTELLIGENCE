# Screening

Know what deserves attention before you spend time underwriting it. Every opportunity is evaluated against your firm's criteria — and the result is an explanation, not a score.

AI explains why the opportunity fits, where it does not, and what remains unknown. It never reduces a deal to a number.

## Pipeline

Deal → Rules → Analysis → Red flags → Unknown → Fit → Advance / Pass

## Capabilities

- **Financial fit** — revenue, EBITDA, margins, growth, cash flow, and leverage assessed against the thresholds your firm defines.
- **Market fit** — market growth, fragmentation, competition, cyclicality, and disruption risk surfaced from research and documents.
- **Business quality** — recurring revenue, retention, pricing power, customer concentration, moat, and growth levers evaluated qualitatively and quantitatively.
- **Strategic fit** — sector, geography, transaction size, and ownership structure matched against your investment mandate.
- **Red flags** — issues that would change the underwriting path surfaced early, each linked to the evidence that raised it.
- **Unknowns** — what you do not know yet is listed explicitly; the output is honest about information gaps.
- **Criteria as rules** — your investment criteria become machine-readable rules applied consistently across every deal.
- **Advance or pass** — the output is a recommendation to advance, pass, or gather more information, always with reasoning a human can follow.

## The principle

The goal is not a score. It is a clear explanation of fit, risk, and what remains unknown — so the team can decide where to spend its time.
