# Due Diligence

Turn diligence into structured investment intelligence. A large body of unstructured material becomes organized workstreams, findings, risks, owners, and deadlines — a decision system, not a pile of PDFs.

The value is not uploading documents and asking questions. It is converting a large body of unstructured diligence material into a structured decision system.

## Pipeline

Request → Document → Extraction → Evidence → Finding → Risk → Question → Resolution → IC

## Capabilities

- **Workstreams** — financial, commercial, legal, tax, operational, technology, HR, and environmental where relevant — organized the way your diligence runs.
- **Request lists** — diligence requests tracked per workstream, with owners, deadlines, and status.
- **Document classification** — documents classified and routed to the right workstream automatically.
- **Extraction** — key facts, figures, and provisions extracted and linked to the document and page they came from.
- **Evidence linking** — every finding references its source document and location, so nothing is asserted without provenance.
- **Findings & risks** — findings, risks, contradictions, and missing information surfaced with owners and resolution status.
- **Q&A** — questions tracked with answers and evidence, resolved or escalated as the timeline requires.
- **Diligence summaries** — a structured diligence summary assembled for the investment committee, workstream by workstream.

## The principle

Diligence should produce a structured picture of risk, not a searchable pile of documents.
