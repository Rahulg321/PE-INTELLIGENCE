# Transaction Execution

Execute from LOI through closing with control. Milestones, approvals, advisors, financing, legal workflow, and closing conditions are tracked in one place — alongside the full diligence and underwriting record.

The platform supports execution and coordination. It does not replace legal counsel or transaction advisors.

## Pipeline

LOI → Diligence → Negotiation → Financing → Legal → Approvals → Closing → Post-close

## Capabilities

- **Transaction checklist** — every step from LOI to close is tracked with status and owners.
- **Deal milestones** — key dates and milestones visible to the whole deal team.
- **Document tracking** — execution versions, signature status, and document flow tracked without losing history.
- **Approvals** — internal approvals and their evidence recorded as the transaction advances.
- **Advisors** — legal, financial, tax, and other advisors tracked with their deliverables and deadlines.
- **Financing** — financing steps and conditions tracked alongside the transaction.
- **Closing conditions** — closing conditions tracked to completion, each tied to its source.
- **Post-close handover** — the full record carries into portfolio monitoring; nothing is lost at close.

## The principle

Execution is coordination. Give the team one view of every step, owner, and condition — and let the advisors do the advising.
