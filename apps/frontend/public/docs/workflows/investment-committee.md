# Investment Committee

Give decision-makers the context behind the deal. The IC package brings together the company, thesis, financials, valuation, structure, management, market, diligence, risks, and returns — every conclusion traceable to its evidence.

Every important conclusion should be traceable to its evidence. AI assists in preparing the IC. It does not make the investment decision.

## Pipeline

Underwriting → Diligence → IC package → Evidence → Risks → Mitigants → Returns → Decision

## Capabilities

- **Company overview** — the business, market, and model summarized from the full record.
- **Investment thesis** — the case for the investment, stated clearly and tied to its assumptions.
- **Financial performance** — historical and projected performance with the key drivers called out.
- **Valuation & structure** — valuation, transaction structure, financing, and returns across scenarios.
- **Management & market** — management assessment and market context, each with its evidence.
- **Diligence & risks** — workstream findings, risks, mitigants, and open questions in one view.
- **Base, upside, downside** — returns presented across cases so the decision spans scenarios, not a point estimate.
- **Evidence trace** — each conclusion can be opened back to its source document, page, and section.

## The principle

The committee should be able to ask "where does that come from?" and get an answer in one click.
