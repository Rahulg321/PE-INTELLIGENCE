# Investment Workflows

A configurable investment lifecycle, built around the way investment teams work. Each stage is supported by the platform and connects to the one before it.

## Origination

Capture opportunities without forcing your team to manually re-enter information. Existing systems become inputs; fragmented deal flow becomes structured companies, deals, and relationships.

## Screening

Evaluate every opportunity against your firm's criteria — and understand why it fits, where it does not, and what remains unknown. The output is an explanation, not a score.

## Underwriting

Build the investment case from complete context — company, financials, market, management, transaction, and valuation — so the thesis is a living document traceable to its sources.

## Due Diligence

Turn a large body of unstructured material into organized workstreams, findings, risks, owners, and deadlines. A decision system, not a pile of PDFs.

## Investment Committee

Give decision-makers the context behind the deal. The IC package assembles the thesis, financials, valuation, structure, risks, and returns — every conclusion traceable to its evidence.

## Transaction Execution

Execute from LOI through closing with control. Milestones, approvals, advisors, financing, legal workflow, and closing conditions tracked in one place.

## Portfolio / Value Creation

The underwritten case stays the baseline after closing. Actual performance is measured against it, so monitoring, value creation, and exit are one continuous thread.
